libsndfile 1.2.2 — LGPL-2.1-or-later (libsndfile/COPYING)

Bundled unmodified as _soundfile_data/libsndfile_x86_64.so, from the soundfile
0.14.0 wheel, which the add-on uses to read audio. It is linked dynamically, so
it can be replaced by another libsndfile 1.x build.

commit 39f3e270a4649576c72630db081658524ce7bea8 (sha256 c872fb84af2afc0e...),
built by that commit's linux_build.sh, with these linked in statically:
